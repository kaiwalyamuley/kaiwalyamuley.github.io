Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

AfterHours Streetwear Co. ("AfterHours", "we") runs this online store. This page explains what information we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your order details. When you order, we collect your name, mobile number, delivery address, any note you add, and what you bought.
  • Messages you send us. When you contact us through the Help page, we keep your message, your topic, and any order number or phone number you give us, so we can reply.
  • Your bag. The items in your bag are saved in your own browser so they are still there when you come back.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you your orders.
  • To print your order, deliver it, and contact you about it, including by phone or WhatsApp.
  • To confirm your UPI payment or collect cash on delivery, process refunds, and keep records of our sales. If you pay by UPI, we keep the reference number you give us. We do not receive your UPI PIN or bank login.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your Google account data for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase stores your account and order information for us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google provides the sign-in service.
  • Courier and delivery partners receive your name, mobile number and address so they can deliver your order.

Printing partners receive the design and size of your tee. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is open and as long as we need order records for tax, accounting or legal reasons.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information. You can also remove AfterHours from your Google account connections at any time. Some order records may need to be kept where the law requires it.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, reach us through our Help page.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
